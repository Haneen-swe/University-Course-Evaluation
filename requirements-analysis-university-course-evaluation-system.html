1 · Introduction & Project Scope

The University Course Evaluation System (UCES) is a web-based platform that enables students to submit structured, anonymous evaluations of courses and instructors at the end of each academic term. The collected data is aggregated and made available to academic administrators, department heads, and (optionally) faculty members to drive continuous improvement in course quality and teaching effectiveness.

This document captures the complete set of functional, non-functional, and constraint requirements, identifies all stakeholders, defines the system boundary, and provides use-case coverage sufficient to move to system design.

The system shall comply with academic data-privacy regulations (e.g., FERPA in the US, PDPA equivalents) and must guarantee the anonymity of all student responses before any data is persisted or queried.

3 · Functional Requirements

FR = Functional Requirement. Priority: High / Medium / Low.

3.1 Authentication & User Management

FR-01User LoginHigh

The system shall authenticate students, instructors, and administrators via institutional Single Sign-On (SSO) or username/password credentials secured with hashed storage.

FR-02Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)High

The system shall enforce four roles: Student, Instructor, Department Head, and Admin. Each role exposes only its permitted views and actions.

FR-03Password RecoveryMedium

The system shall support secure email-based password reset with time-limited tokens (≤ 60 minutes).

FR-04User ProvisioningMedium

Administrators shall be able to create, edit, deactivate, and delete user accounts. Bulk import via CSV shall be supported.

3.2 Course & Enrollment Management

FR-05Course CatalogueHigh

Administrators shall be able to create, update, and archive courses with attributes: course code, title, credits, department, and assigned instructor(s).

FR-06Term / Semester ManagementHigh

The system shall support academic term records (e.g., Fall 2024). Each course offering is linked to exactly one term.

FR-07Student EnrollmentHigh

Students shall be enrolled in course offerings. Only enrolled students may evaluate a given course offering. Enrollment data may be imported from SIS (Student Information System) via API or CSV.

3.3 Evaluation Form Builder

FR-08Form Template ManagementHigh

Administrators shall be able to design evaluation form templates composed of configurable question blocks. A template is reusable across multiple course offerings.

FR-09Question TypesHigh

The system shall support at minimum: (a) Likert scale (1–5 or 1–10), (b) multiple-choice (single answer), (c) multiple-choice (multi-select), and (d) open-ended text.

FR-10Mandatory vs Optional QuestionsMedium

Form designers shall be able to mark any question as mandatory. The submission workflow shall enforce completion of all mandatory questions.

FR-11Evaluation Window ConfigurationHigh

Administrators shall set an open/close date-time window per course offering. Outside this window, submission is not permitted.

3.4 Evaluation Submission

FR-12Anonymous SubmissionHigh

Student identity shall be decoupled from response data at the point of storage. A one-way token records participation status (to prevent duplicate submission) without linking the token to the response content.

FR-13Draft & ResumeMedium

Students shall be able to save a partial response as a draft and resume submission within the evaluation window.

FR-14Duplicate Submission PreventionHigh

Each enrolled student may submit exactly one final evaluation per course offering. Attempts to re-submit shall be rejected after final submission.

FR-15Submission ConfirmationMedium

Upon successful final submission, the student shall receive an in-app confirmation and optional email receipt (containing no response content).

3.5 Results & Analytics

FR-16Aggregated Course ReportHigh

The system shall generate per-course-offering reports showing: response rate, mean/median/standard deviation per Likert question, frequency distribution charts, and open-text responses (anonymised).

FR-17Instructor Summary ReportHigh

Department heads and admins shall view aggregated instructor performance across all their course offerings for a selected term.

FR-18Department & Institution DashboardHigh

A role-appropriate dashboard shall display KPIs: overall response rate, top/bottom-rated courses, trend lines across terms, and department comparison tables.

FR-19ExportMedium

Reports shall be exportable as PDF and CSV. Open-text responses shall be excluded from CSV exports by default to reduce re-identification risk.

FR-20Result Release ControlHigh

Admins shall control whether instructors can view their own results, and optionally enforce a minimum response threshold (e.g., ≥ 5 responses) before results are revealed.

3.6 Notifications

FR-21Evaluation Open NotificationMedium

The system shall send an automated email/in-app notification to enrolled students when an evaluation window opens.

FR-22Reminder NotificationLow

Configurable reminders shall be sent to students who have not yet submitted, at a configurable number of days before window close.

3.7 Audit & Compliance

FR-23Audit LogHigh

All administrative actions (user changes, form edits, window modifications, result releases) shall be persisted in a tamper-evident audit log with timestamp and actor identity.

FR-24Data Retention PolicyMedium

Admins shall configure retention periods per data category. The system shall support scheduled anonymisation or deletion of expired data.

4 · Non-Functional Requirements

NFR = Non-Functional Requirement.
NFR-01PerformanceHigh

Page load time shall not exceed 2 seconds under normal load. The system shall handle at least 1,000 concurrent evaluation submissions without degradation.

NFR-02AvailabilityHigh

The system shall target 99.5% uptime, especially during evaluation windows. Scheduled maintenance shall be performed outside peak periods.

NFR-03SecurityHigh

All data in transit shall use TLS 1.2+. Passwords shall be hashed with bcrypt (cost ≥ 12). The application shall be hardened against OWASP Top-10 vulnerabilities (SQL injection, XSS, CSRF, broken auth, etc.).

NFR-04Privacy & AnonymityHigh

Student response data and student identity shall be stored in logically separated tables/schemas. No query path shall allow correlation of a specific response to a specific student after submission is finalised.

NFR-05ScalabilityMedium

The architecture shall be horizontally scalable (stateless application tier, shared database tier) to support institutions with up to 50,000 enrolled students.

NFR-06UsabilityHigh

The UI shall be responsive (mobile-first), accessible (WCAG 2.1 AA), and operable in modern browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge — last 2 major versions).

NFR-07MaintainabilityMedium

Code shall follow documented coding standards. Unit test coverage shall be ≥ 80% for business-logic modules. API endpoints shall be documented via OpenAPI 3.0.

NFR-08InternationalisationLow

The UI framework shall support i18n from day one; initial language is English. Arabic support shall be achievable without architectural changes.

NFR-09Audit IntegrityMedium

Audit log records shall be append-only; no update or delete operation shall be permitted on the audit table by the application role.

6 · Constraints & Boundary Conditions

CON-01Technology Stack

The system shall be implemented as a web application. No native mobile app is in scope for v1.0.

CON-02Data Residency

All data shall remain within the institution's own server infrastructure or a compliant cloud region as mandated by the institution.

CON-03Anonymity Guarantee

No feature shall be built that allows any user — including system administrators — to reverse-map a submitted response to a specific student after finalisation.

CON-04Minimum Response Threshold

Results for a course shall not be visible to the assigned instructor until the configurable minimum response count is met, to further protect anonymity.

CON-05Academic Calendar Dependency

Evaluation windows are tied to the academic calendar. The system shall not auto-open windows; manual or scheduled activation by an admin is required.

7 · Assumptions

  • The university has an existing directory service (LDAP / Active Directory or OAuth provider) that UCES can integrate with for SSO.
  • A Student Information System (SIS) exists and can provide enrollment data via CSV export or REST API.
  • All users have access to institutional email addresses for notifications and password recovery.
  • The initial deployment targets a single institution; multi-tenancy is a future enhancement.
  • Evaluation forms are designed by admins; the system does not use AI to auto-generate questions (Responsible AI boundary).
  • Open-text feedback will not be processed by AI sentiment analysis in v1.0 to avoid privacy and bias risks.

8 · Responsible AI Considerations

This section documents AI-risk mitigations applicable to UCES to ensure ethical, fair, and transparent operation.
PrincipleApplication to UCES
PrivacyStudent responses are anonymised at storage time. No analytics query can identify individual respondents.
FairnessEvaluation forms apply uniformly to all courses. Minimum response thresholds prevent statistically unreliable (and potentially biased) results from influencing instructor reviews.
TransparencyStudents are informed at submission time what their data will be used for and who can access aggregated results.
AccountabilityAll administrative actions are logged in a tamper-evident audit trail. Any result release decision is attributed to a named admin.
Non-maleficenceAI-driven sentiment analysis or performance scoring of instructors is explicitly out of scope for v1.0 to prevent algorithmic bias affecting employment decisions.
Data MinimisationOnly data strictly necessary for evaluation purposes is collected. No tracking of device fingerprints, IP addresses, or session behaviour beyond authentication needs.
